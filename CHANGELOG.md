## [1.1.0] - 2026-10-26

### Added
- Bank-statement style customer history: previous balance, +/- entry, new balance, grouped by date.
- Entry animations, count-up balances and press feedback.

### Changed
- Premium visual redesign: plum gradient header, elevated cards, balance hero cards.
- Larger quick-quantity chips and stepper on the Sales screen.

### Fixed
- Header overlapping the status bar on Android 15.
