## What it does

Kali Dinali is a billing and inventory app for a small shop, made for counters with weak or no mobile signal.

- **Sell by box or loose unit.** Take part payments and keep the balance.
- **Stock in boxes**, with loose units tracked automatically.
- **Customer dues (udhaar) and payments** in a simple customer ledger.
- **App lock screen** plus **backup and restore**.
- **Fully offline.** All data stays in a local database on the phone.

## How it works

1. **Set up products:** box price, loose price and units per box. You only do this once.
2. **Record a sale:** pick the customer and the product, then enter what was paid now.
3. **Track the rest:** stock drops and the customer's due updates instantly.

## Install

1. Download [`app-release.apk`](https://github.com/Hrishank21s/kali-dinali-releases/releases/latest/download/app-release.apk) on your Android phone.
2. Open it. You may need to allow "Install unknown apps" for your browser or file manager once.
3. Install and open **Kali Dinali**.

To update, install the newer APK over the old one. Back up your data first from the Backup screen.
